The Journey of Loops

Where it all began,
and why I never stopped.

My crochet journey started way back when I was in class 10. Holding a hook and turning a simple ball of yarn into something tangible felt like pure magic. It was my cozy escape amidst school days, filled with endless curiosity and dropped stitches.

✨ Class 10 Beginnings

First learned the basic stitches and fell completely in love with creating handmade treasures from scratch.

🌿 A Quiet Pause

Stepped away for a certain time as life got busy, keeping the passion tucked safely in my heart.

Coming back to crochet and turning it into CROCHETWITHPOOH turned out to be the absolute best decision I've ever made. Every single stitch today carries a piece of that childhood love, grown into something much more personal.

Thank you for being part of my cozy little chapter. Every piece I make is stitched slowly so it can stay with you forever! 🌸
